【How to review core flows】
1. Sign in with the demo account above.
2. Create a site (現場) from the home list (+).
3. Upload a drawing (図面) from album or camera; calibrate scale if prompted.
4. Measure walls / ceiling on the canvas; tap the red number badge to open materials / settings.
5. Free plan: 1 drawing upload per month base (invite bonus can raise to max 3). Paid seat packs unlock unlimited uploads.
6. Account → purchase a seat pack (Sandbox) to verify IAP; use Restore Purchases if needed.
7. Account → アカウントを削除 / Delete account is available (password confirmation). Apple subscriptions must be cancelled separately in Settings → Subscriptions.
